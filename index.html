Über mich

Mein Name ist Tuncay ERGİN. Ich bin seit 20 Jahren als Sozialarbeits-Spezialist tätig und habe in dieser Zeit hunderte Familien mit älteren und behinderten Angehörigen in schwierigen Lebenssituationen begleitet.

About Me

My name is Tuncay ERGİN. I have worked as a Social Work Specialist for 20 years, guiding hundreds of families with elderly and disabled relatives through challenging life situations.

For 15 years, I served as Responsible Manager of a private care center for people with disabilities in Turkey. This experience gave me a deep understanding of care, legal, and human needs.

Today I support expats and local lawyers in ensuring that elderly and disabled relatives and clients in Turkey receive dignified, safe, and legally correct care – and I advise investors on establishing and operating private care centers.

À propos de moi

Je m'appelle Tuncay ERGİN. Je travaille depuis 20 ans en tant que spécialiste du travail social et j'ai accompagné des centaines de familles ayant des proches âgés ou handicapés dans des situations de vie difficiles.
